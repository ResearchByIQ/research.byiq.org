The problem

At a trillion files, traversal dies.

The standard way to know what is in a filesystem is to walk it. Across a collection of filesystems holding trillions of file nodes, a walk never finishes — inventory is always out of date, movement is guesswork, and policy is applied to a map that no longer matches the territory.

The primitives were designed for one tree of manageable size. The problem is now many trees, each beyond the size where enumeration is a viable operation.

The approach

Operate without enumerating.

01

Inventory without traversal

Datagator maintains a live picture of collections of filesystems without walking them node by node, so knowing what exists does not require an operation that cannot complete.

02

Movement and policy at scale

Data movement and retention, access and placement policy are expressed and enforced across the whole collection at once, rather than file by file — the unit of work is the collection, not the node.
